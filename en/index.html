MY POSITIONING

The executive assistant role is evolving. I want to contribute to that evolution.

Anticipating, coordinating, filtering information and protecting decision-makers’ time remain at the heart of the role. Artificial intelligence now adds new levers: faster synthesis, better preparation, clearer follow-up and less repetitive work.

My approach is to bring these two cultures together rather than oppose them: the rigour of executive assistance and the power of new tools.
