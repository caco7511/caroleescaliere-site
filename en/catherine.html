CATHERINE · AI ASSISTANT

Less admin. More time to decide.

Catherine explores a new way to support leaders by combining executive-assistance methods with artificial intelligence to organise information, prepare the day and automate tasks that can safely be delegated.

Talk about CatherineAll projects

THE PRINCIPLE

AI that assists without deciding for you.

01

Prioritise

Surface the information and actions that matter so the day starts with a clearer view of priorities.

02

Prepare

Turn emails, calendar items and available information into useful outputs such as summaries, drafts and next-action preparation.

03

Automate

Reduce repetitive work while keeping human validation whenever an action commits the user.

VISION

Technology in service of attention.

The goal is not to replace trust, context or judgement. Catherine is designed to free time spent collecting, sorting and preparing information so that attention can return to decisions, people and priorities.

The project is being developed and tested progressively around practical professional use cases.
