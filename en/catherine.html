CATHERINE · AI ASSISTANT

Less admin. More time to decide.

Catherine explores a new way to support leaders by combining executive-assistance methods with artificial intelligence to organise information, prepare the day and automate tasks that can safely be delegated.

Talk about CatherineSee use cases

THE PRINCIPLE

AI that assists without deciding for you.

01

Prioritise

Surface the information and actions that matter so the day starts with a clearer view of priorities.

02

Prepare

Turn emails, calendar items and available information into useful outputs such as summaries, drafts and next-action preparation.

03

Automate

Reduce repetitive work while keeping human validation whenever an action commits the user.

USE CASES

What can Catherine actually help with?

Morning brief

Start with what matters

Bring together meetings, messages and important topics to create a concise overview of the day's priorities.

Email

Sort and prepare

Identify important messages, summarise them and prepare replies where appropriate, with validation before sending.

Calendar

Prepare for meetings

Add context to upcoming meetings and surface useful information to make preparation easier.

Follow-up

Keep track of next actions

Turn incoming information into actions to follow and help maintain a clear view of what still needs to be done.

IN DEVELOPMENT

A product built step by step.

Catherine is currently a project in development and experimentation. Connections, use cases and automations are tested progressively before being generalised.

The principle remains constant: users retain control over their data and important actions. Sensitive functions should rely on explicit permissions and appropriate human validation.

VISION

Technology in service of attention.

The goal is not to replace trust, context or judgement. Catherine is designed to free time spent collecting, sorting and preparing information so that attention can return to decisions, people and priorities.
